# Changelog
## Unreleased
- Floor markings and walls traced from a plan with many rooms (a parking) are filled in 3D again: the triangulation of a shape with several holes joined the wrong copy of a repeated corner and gave up (only the sides showed). Markings whose outline crosses itself are traced again before they are drawn.
- Import wizard: straightening a picture is much faster (the file is decoded once, the PNG preview is written with light compression and the gray pixels come from the same pass) and runs in the background with a progress bar; the tests no longer depend on the regional settings of the machine.
- Expert mode hides the step bar and "Next step"; Align and Stretch (calibrating a plan on the level below) are expert tools. The background panel keeps only Show and opacity: the numeric scale/rotation/offset fields and "Difference mode" are removed.
- Wall tool: Space changes the side (left / centre / right) of the segment being drawn and of the next ones, no longer of the whole chain already placed. The wall display button (solid / marks / wireframe) sits under the view cube and shows in the 2D plan only.
- Riser tool: Space turns the pipes even when a combo box of the options still has the focus.
- Select tool: "Polygon selection" option (click the corners, Enter or double-click to close). Cut tool: "Split into pieces" option (a line cuts walls and paint into two or more pieces, nothing is removed).
- File › Allow AI Agents (MCP) and File › Connect an AI agent…: an MCP server (Streamable HTTP, loopback only, port 39017) edits the building open in the window with the same history; the dialog gives the `claude mcp add` command and the JSON configuration.
- New line icons for the tool strip and undo / redo (drawn from geometries, they follow the theme).
- The Elevation button (front view of a selected wall, and the whole-building front/right/back/left views of the elevation control) is removed with its code. The side views of the plan (2D view cube) stay.
- Walk mode: door frames (jambs, lintel, window sill) in the 3D views and the door numbers or labels floating above the doors; the camera comes down from above when entering the walk; "Show in plan" and "Show in 3D" on the selection (over the walk view, in the plan and in the 3D view). One opening geometry in Core is now shared by the plan, the elevation, the 3D mesh and the walk.
- macOS package, as for Kubism: the native libraries are thinned to the package's architecture (they were universal, which triggers the "Intel-based app" warning), the app bundle is ad-hoc signed (Apple silicon refuses unsigned code), a complete Info.plist, the architectures and the signature are checked at packaging time and in CI; the disk image has the Applications shortcut and a first-launch readme. Pre-releases (tags with a hyphen) are flagged as such. The Intel macOS package (osx-x64) is no longer built in the releases.

## v0.1.0-rc11 (2026-10-31)
- Walk mode (G, or the Walk button): first-person view at eye height through the corridors of a level, to see the pipes and objects where they are. Drag to look, click the floor to glide there, arrows or Z Q S D (physical keys) to move, E and A for the eye height, Page Up/Down or the level bar to change level. Walls, floors and ceilings are drawn in perspective (OpenGL only); doors and windows cut the 3D walls. Ghost mode (X) lets you cross walls, dissolves the walls around you and shows the pipes inside them dimmed. A map of the level, the room you are in, hover labels and a legend of the networks are drawn over the view. The world limit is automatic, or set from the visible plan (View menu). Read-only: nothing is recorded in the history.
- Room tool: the Options tab has "Openings closed — up to … mm" (default 2000, 0 = only fully closed rooms, kept in the settings). Openings up to that width are closed whatever the position of the cursor in the room (it used to depend on how far the cursor was from a wall), so a room with wide doors or bays gets its own preview instead of merging with the hall behind them.
- Walk mode: room names float in the view (key R or the Names button, on by default): the name you typed, or the kind ("Bike room") when the name was generated, with the room number and a colour per kind; only rooms in front of you, near enough and not behind a wall (ghost mode sees through). The map shows the names and lights the room you are in.
- Floor markings: parking bay lines that the scan traced as thin walls can become paint on the floor (flat white shapes, no longer walls: they block nothing, do not cut rooms). New tool "Paint on the floor" (B): drag a zone, check the orange preview, Enter; the thin material (under 150 mm by default) becomes paint while real walls and pillars stay walls; modes to turn paint back into walls or erase it. Also an action on the selected walls and an option in the import wizard. Drawn white in the plan, in 3D and when walking; exported on its own DXF layer.
- Floor markings can be selected (click, Shift+click or box, together with the walls), turned back into walls ("Back to wall"), deleted or moved; the cut tool cuts the paint too, and only the selection when there is one.

## Up to v0.1.0-rc10
- Core model, history, actions, `.immeuble` file format; Plan detection/straightening; Scene meshes; Avalonia shell with plan view; MCP server (levels, riser, BOM, undo/redo).
- Keyboard shortcuts (undo/redo, select all, copy/cut/paste/duplicate, save/open/new/close/export, zoom, tools V/W/P/D/N/S/M, Delete, Escape, F9).
- Plan: adaptive grid with scale bar, snap to grid (F9), origin marker, Space+drag / trackpad pan, Ctrl(⌘)+drag rotation, riser and door/window selection with properties, wall alignment (centre/left/right, Space) with dashed preview.
- 3D: orientation view cube. Properties panel scrolls.
- Import wizard: zoom/pan (wheel, Space+drag, trackpad), alignment guides, pick two points along a wall to make it horizontal/vertical, live rotation preview.
- Import wizard: ±0.1° rotation buttons, scale and position taken from an already imported level.
- Plan: dashed previews before placing doors, windows, risers, palette objects and rooms; rooms can be selected, renamed and deleted; Delete now works on pipes, risers and equipment.
- Riser templates "cold + hot + hot return" and "drainage only" (new Hot water return network type).
- Front / right / back / left views of the whole building (never from below), with pipe drawing at a chosen depth.
- Plan: snap keeps the grid along straight lines and its reach follows the zoom; a green ring + cross with the tool name shows where the next click lands; dashed ghosts for every palette object (valves on pipes included).
